01 The problem

Most dental and orthodontic practices send aligners, retainers, and trays to an outside lab. That adds cost, shipping time, and a back-and-forth every time something needs to change.

02 The product

Printadontics is desktop software that lets a practice design and 3D-print aligners, trays, retainers, and snore guards in the office. It includes AI tray generation from patient scans, an attachment library, and print-ready export. Practices download it directly; it is not an app-store product.

03 My role

I co-founded the company and led customer discovery. I ran interviews with practicing dentists and orthodontists and turned what they told me about their clinical workflow into product and technical requirements.

04 Where it stands

The software is in validation pilots at three clinics, testing whether it holds up clinically and fits real practice workflows. The old marketing site is no longer online, so this page is the best place to learn about it. Happy to walk through the product on a call.
