01 The problem

Managers of hourly teams juggle schedules, shift swaps, team messages, and time-off requests across group chats and spreadsheets. Things get missed, and the manager is always the bottleneck.

02 The product

Chapshift puts scheduling, shift swaps, team messaging, and time-off requests in one dashboard. It can generate a schedule from each employee’s preferences and availability, so a manager starts from a draft instead of a blank week.

03 My role

  • Built and shipped the app to both the Apple App Store and Google Play.
  • Set product and technical requirements from user interviews and testing, and iterated on the AI features based on what users did.
  • Coordinated engineering and marketing through daily syncs against an external launch deadline.
  • Ran product demos and direct outreach to recruit early adopters.

04 Where it stands

The app is still listed on the Apple App Store. The old marketing site is no longer online.
